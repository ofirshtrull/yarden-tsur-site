Biography

Music shouldn't just tell the story — it should breathe life into it.

  • Animation & children's music
  • Theatre & musicals
  • Film & TV scoring
  • Pop songwriting & production
  • Orchestration

Yarden “Denny” Tsur is an Israeli-American composer, songwriter, and producer based in Atlanta. She writes music that carries a story — from the playful worlds of animation and children's music to cinematic scores and radio-ready pop.

Today her music reaches a global audience: she scores animated television series broadcast in markets around the world, writing songs and themes that travel across languages and cultures. Her original score for a stage production of Winnie the Pooh turned beloved characters into full musical numbers, and her short-film soundtracks give each picture its emotional spine.

A finalist in Israel's 2013 Eurovision selection, she's as at home on an international stage as she is in the writing room. Whatever the brief, Denny builds music with real craft and a little bit of magic — fresh, character-driven, and made to fit the exact moment it scores.
